# Credits and attributions

SCS Companion is independent community software. It is not affiliated with or endorsed by the organizations and projects below.

## Hardware and protocol research

- **Stanton Magnetics** and the original **SCS.3d / DaScratch** hardware and **DaRouter** teams created the device and its original software ecosystem.
- **Sean M. Pappalardo (`Pegasus-RPG`)** authored the mature Mixxx SCS.3d controller script that served as an important behavioral and LED-protocol reference. Later maintainers include **Christian (`git-developer`)**, **Swiftb0y**, **Jan Holthuis (`Holzhaus`)**, **Owen Williams (`ywwg`)**, and other [Mixxx contributors](https://github.com/mixxxdj/mixxx/commits/main/res/controllers/Stanton-SCS3d-scripts.js).
- **ritola** created [Oneten Router](https://github.com/ritola/onetenrouter), an independent open-source SCS.3d router experiment used as background research.

## Concepts and software

- **Neil Thapen** created [Pink Trombone](https://dood.al/pinktrombone/), the vocal-tract synthesis concept that inspired the native vocal module.
- **Korg** created the KAOSSILATOR family and its scale-locked touch-performance workflow, which inspired the Kaoss module.
- **Mark Heath and the NAudio contributors** created [NAudio](https://github.com/naudio/NAudio), used under the MIT License for Windows audio I/O and processing.
- **Microsoft and Windows App SDK / WinUI contributors** provide the Windows desktop platform and UI framework.
- The **Mixxx**, **VirtualDJ**, **VRChat OSC**, **Audacity**, and **Discord** projects, teams, documentation authors, and communities informed their respective companion modules.
- **OpenAI and GPT-5.6 Sol** collaborated on implementation.
